# Eval CT

## Purpose

Score CT performance consistently across runs.

## Required Metrics

- Constraint compliance rate
- Unsupported claim rate
- Drift event count
- Evidence coverage
- Hypothesis testability score
- Experiment readiness score
- Contradiction resolution rate
- Friction reduction score

## Required Harness Outputs

- `cases.yaml`
- `run-config.yaml`
- `rubric.yaml`
- `results.jsonl`
- `analysis.md`
